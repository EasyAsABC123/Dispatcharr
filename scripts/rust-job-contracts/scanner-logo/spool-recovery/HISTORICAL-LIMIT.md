This directory preserves the exact earlier770 reconstruction, tools and captured gates. Original Dispatcharr and dependency authors retain full credit.

A separate deterministic diagnostic confirmed that an unrelated child can inherit the descriptor before exec, extending the flock lifetime. The historical failure itself lacked enough diagnostics to retrospectively prove its exact cause.

Use the separate `../spool-recovery-unlock/` recipe for the subsequent explicit-unlock correction and its actual fork/process/worker evidence.
