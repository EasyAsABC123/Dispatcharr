Read the actual saved report on both sides and compare every field and type under that real representation. Preserve any failed controller rather than relabeling it after an offline leaf review.

Keep physical diagnostic provenance separate from business/content equality. FFmpeg diagnostic pointers and interleaved independent-stream warnings changed across real processes, while complete framehash content and all window/certificate checks matched. Preserve both literal stderr artifacts, state the precise observed difference and keep the original warning/outer-cut gates. Do not introduce generic stderr, timestamp, container or content scrubbing. A new warning text requires investigation.

Target controls at the actual optimization boundary: same-size different bytes, tool/image/options drift, failed/incomplete authority, altered stdout/stderr/receipt and unsafe input paths. Then execute the smallest owned real decoder qualification with exact CID/process retirement. A verifier repair does not justify a broad live measurement rerun, acceptance of a valid subset from a failed campaign, or retroactive resource subtraction.
